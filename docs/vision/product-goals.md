# Product Goals

Alloy should:

1. Capture engineering intent in a structured model that can be queried,
   versioned, validated, and compiled into runtime guidance.
2. Help developers express tacit engineering knowledge through
   [[intent-capture|assistant-led elicitation]] rather than blank-form entry.
3. Extract likely engineering intent from existing codebases as hypotheses,
   then let humans confirm, reject, or refine those hypotheses.
4. Compile relevant product, design, and engineering intent into
   [[alloy-and-foundry|Foundry]] [[formation-brief|formation briefs]].
5. Generate [[prompt-pack|prompt packs]] tailored to Foundry phases such as
   assessment, triage, planning, execution, retry, and summarization.
6. Generate or select evidence requirements and gates that prove whether the
   engineering intent is satisfied.
7. Feed Foundry trace outcomes back into the intent model.
8. Keep Foundry's event and task-block runtime clean by moving situational
   policy into Alloy.
9. Preserve [[alloy-and-epilogue-tracker|Epilogue Tracker]]'s product focus by
   keeping technical guidance out of the backlog while still making it available
   to tools.
