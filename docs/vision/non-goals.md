# Non-Goals

Alloy should not:

1. Replace [[alloy-and-foundry|Foundry]] as the engineering workflow runtime.
2. Replace [[alloy-and-epilogue-tracker|Epilogue Tracker]] as the product intent
   system.
3. Replace a [[design-intent-system|design intent system]].
4. Become a general-purpose project-management or ticketing tool.
5. Become Jira with engineering-sounding nouns.
6. Become a static ADR repository where records are written once and forgotten.
7. Treat principles as commandments without tradeoffs.
8. Automatically accept codebase inferences without human validation.
9. Force every development team into the same architecture.
10. Encode all knowledge into `CHARTER.md`; repo-local documents should be
    projections of the intent graph, not necessarily the canonical store.
